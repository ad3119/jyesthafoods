Who we are

A rice house built around the whole grain

Jyestha Foods Pvt Ltd is a focused rice enterprise in Tumakuru. We combine careful sourcing with modern processing so every shipment — table rice or by-product — arrives clean, consistent and ready to use.

The brief is simple: nutritious rice for healthier tables, and reliable supply for traders and importers.
