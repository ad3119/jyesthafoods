Who We Are

About Jyestha Foods

Jyestha Foods Pvt Ltd is a focused rice industry enterprise engaged in manufacturing, trading and export of premium quality rice and valuable by-products. Rooted in Tumakuru, Karnataka, we combine traditional knowledge of Indian rice varieties with modern processing standards.

Our mission is simple: deliver consistent, hygienic and nutritious rice that supports healthier lifestyles — both in domestic markets and across international borders.
